Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

TruePostcode ("we", "us") provides a free tool for checking publicly available UK flood-risk and crime data by postcode. This policy explains what data is involved when you use the site and what choices you have. We've tried to keep it short and in plain English.

1. The short version

We don't require an account, we don't ask for your name or address, and the postcode you search is sent directly from your browser to the government data sources themselves — it doesn't pass through or get logged on our own servers. The only thing we store locally on your device is your cookie preference and, if you accept cookies, advertising cookies set by our ad partner.

2. What data we collect

Postcode searches. When you enter a postcode, your browser queries postcodes.io, data.police.uk, the Environment Agency's public data services, HM Land Registry, and OpenStreetMap's Overpass API directly. We do not operate a backend server that receives, logs, or stores the postcodes you search. Those third parties may retain their own standard server logs (e.g. IP address) under their own privacy policies — see section 4.

Cookies and local storage. We use your browser's local storage to remember your cookie consent choice. If you accept, we also allow cookies from Google AdSense (see section 3). No cookies are set before you make a choice.

Basic analytics (if enabled). If we add privacy-focused analytics in future (e.g. to see which pages are popular), we will update this policy first and it will not involve cross-site tracking or personal identifiers.

3. Advertising and cookies

TruePostcode is funded by advertising rather than by paid accounts, which is what lets us keep every report free. If you accept cookies, we may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices.
  • If you select "Necessary only" in our cookie banner, no advertising cookies are set and you'll see either no ads or non-personalised ads only.

You can change your choice at any time using the "Cookie settings" link in the site footer.

4. Third-party data sources

All property, flood and crime data shown comes directly from official UK public sources, queried live from your browser:

  • postcodes.io — postcode geocoding (Office for National Statistics data, Open Government Licence)
  • data.police.uk — street-level crime data (Home Office / individual police forces, Open Government Licence)
  • environment.data.gov.uk — flood risk and flood warnings (Environment Agency, Open Government Licence)
  • landregistry.data.gov.uk — sold-price history (HM Land Registry, Open Government Licence)
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass API) — nearby schools, stations and amenities (OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database Licence — a different licence from the OGL sources above, with its own share-alike attribution terms)

Each operates under its own privacy policy governing any data your browser sends them directly (typically just your IP address as part of a normal web request). We don't control and aren't responsible for their practices.

5. Your rights

Because we don't collect or store personal data on our own servers, most UK GDPR data-subject rights (access, erasure, portability) don't have anything to act on from our side. If you have accepted advertising cookies and want to know what Google holds about you, use the Google Ads Settings link above. If you have any privacy question or concern, contact us using the details below.

6. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially — for example if we introduce accounts or additional tracking — we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, show a notice on the site.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: truepostcode@gmail.com.
