Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 19 August 2026

We want TruePostcode to be usable by everyone, including people using a keyboard alone, a screen reader, or with low vision. This isn't a legal requirement for a consumer site like this one, but it matters to us — and to anyone evaluating the tool for use on a business or agency site.

Our standard

We work to WCAG 2.1 level AA. Areas we've specifically checked and fixed: colour contrast on status indicators and body text, keyboard reachability of every interactive control (including the example-postcode buttons), screen-reader announcements for asynchronous loading states (via aria-live regions), and proper heading structure so the report's sections can be navigated by heading. Modals use the native <dialog> element for correct focus-trapping, and the site respects prefers-reduced-motion.

Known limitations

This is an actively developed product, not a finished, independently audited one. If you encounter a genuine barrier — something you can't complete with a keyboard or screen reader, or text you can't read at your preferred contrast/zoom — we want to know, and we treat these as real bugs, not polish.

Reporting an issue

Contact us at truepostcode@gmail.com with what you were trying to do, the browser/assistive technology you were using, and what went wrong. We'll acknowledge genuine barriers and prioritise fixing them.

This statement describes our current approach and testing, not a certified conformance audit.
