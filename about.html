About TruePostcode

Last updated: 19 August 2026

TruePostcode is a free tool for checking official UK flood-risk and crime data for any postcode, built because that data already exists in public government databases but is scattered across separate services, written in technical language, or bundled behind expensive property reports. We pull it together, translate it into plain English, and show it directly — no account, no paywall on the core report.

What we show

Long-term flood risk and live flood warnings from the Environment Agency, street-level crime data from data.police.uk, sold-price history from HM Land Registry, official deprivation rankings from the Index of Multiple Deprivation, and nearby schools, stations and amenities from OpenStreetMap. Every figure links back to the official source it came from — see our methodology notes on the main site for how each one is calculated.

How this is funded

The core report is, and will stay, free. We fund the site through a one-off Pro PDF export (currently £4.99) for anyone who wants a formatted, shareable document — useful for estate agents, landlords, or buyers who want to forward a report — and, going forward, through non-intrusive advertising shown alongside free reports. We don't sell or resell the data you look up, and postcode searches aren't logged on our servers; see our privacy policy for the full detail.

Who's behind it

TruePostcode is an independently run UK project. It's a young, small operation — if you spot something wrong with the data, the site, or how something is explained, we'd genuinely like to hear about it. Contact details are below.

Limitations, honestly stated

We display government and OpenStreetMap data as published, without independently verifying it. Crime figures typically lag by about two months and cover a wide radius rather than an exact address. Flood-risk bands are long-term estimates, not a guarantee for any individual property. None of this is a substitute for a professional survey, conveyancing search, or financial/legal advice — see our terms of use for the full position.
