Common questions

Is TruePostcode really free?

Yes — the full report (flood risk, crime, sold prices, deprivation ranking, schools, stations and amenities) is free with no account and no limit on searches. We offer an optional one-off £4.99 PDF export for anyone who wants a formatted, shareable document, and the site is also supported by advertising.

Where does the data come from?

Directly from official UK sources, queried live in your browser: postcodes.io (ONS postcode data), the Environment Agency (flood risk and warnings), data.police.uk (crime), HM Land Registry (sold prices) and OpenStreetMap (schools, stations, amenities). Nothing is cached or resold — see our About page for the full list.

How accurate is the flood risk shown?

The long-term band comes directly from the Environment Agency's official flood-zone classification and is the same data mortgage lenders and insurers reference — but it's an estimate for the general area, not a certified survey of one specific property. See our methodology notes (in the report, next to "Flood risk") for exactly how each band is calculated.

Does every UK postcode work?

Postcode lookup and sold-price history cover England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. Crime and flood-risk coverage currently focuses on England and Wales, where the underlying government data sources are available in the format we use — the report clearly marks anything not covered for a given postcode rather than showing misleading blanks.

Is my search private?

We don't operate a backend server that logs the postcodes you search — your browser queries the data sources directly. See our privacy policy for the full detail, including how the optional advertising cookies work.

How flood risk is calculated

Long-term risk is the Environment Agency's official flood-zone classification for rivers and the sea — a fixed annual probability band for this location, independent of today's weather:

High≥ 3.3% annual chance
Medium1% – 3.3%
Low0.1% – 1%
Very low< 0.1%

Current status shows whether a warning or alert is active right now, from the EA's real-time monitoring service.
